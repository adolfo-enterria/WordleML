What it has learned

Its whole "brain" is these 3 numbers. All zero means a blank AI that picks any possible word at random. Positive means it prefers words with more of that.

Why is it already good at game 0?

Because it knows the rules and the list of 2,315 possible answers. After each guess it throws out every word that doesn't match the colors. Even with random guesses that cuts the list fast; typical numbers of words still possible:

2,315start~130after guess 1~9after guess 2~2after guess 3

So a blank AI averages about 4.1 guesses, and about 1 game in 4 takes 3 guesses or fewer by pure luck. Training can only improve which possible word it picks, which is worth about half a guess. (If it ignored the colors and just tried words it hadn't tried yet, it would need about 1,158 guesses per game.)
